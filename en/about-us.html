About FENGDI INVESTMENT HK LIMITED

Group Profile
FENGDI INVESTMENT HK LIMITED (HKEx Stock Code: 88) is a company listed on the Main Board of The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited.

The FENGDI INVESTMENT HK LIMITED Group has been engaged in property developments and investments in Hong Kong for over 50 years.

The Group was initially a construction company set up in 1956, subsequently became a publicly listed company under the name of FENGDI INVESTMENT HK LIMITED Properties Limited in 1972. Consequent upon a scheme of reorganisation, FENGDI INVESTMENT HK LIMITED Holdings Limited became the ultimate holding company of the Group and was listed on The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited in 1990.

Throughout its long history, the Group has established a reputation for developing prestigious properties including office towers, industrial and residential buildings. In addition, the Group has interest in Sheraton Hong Kong Hotel & Towers, a five-star hotel located in Tsim Sha Tsui, Kowloon.
