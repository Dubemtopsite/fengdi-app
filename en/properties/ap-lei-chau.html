VELE

VELE is the Group's latest luxurious residential development at the south side of Hong Kong Island. The project consists of 38 residences in 4-bedroom configurations, each with panoramic seaviews and high specifications. 32 units are typical units that span across 16 floors, while there are also 6 simplex and duplex units at the top of the building, some are additionally attached with outdoor activity areas. Each unit is also provided with two parking spaces, making it an appealing addition to the luxurious residential market in Hong Kong.
